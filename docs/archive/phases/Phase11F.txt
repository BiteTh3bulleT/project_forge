Core doctrine:

- Integration readiness is not live integration.
- Adapter contracts must not mutate live state.

- Kernel commit remains canonical truth.

- Retrieval does not make content truth.
- Embeddings, vector indexes, VSA records, and retrieval results are evidence/routing signals, not canonical truth.

Adapters may observe, normalize, mirror, and report.
Adapters may not mutate, execute, commit, approve, route, publish, call model runtimes, or alter user-visible output.

Rules:

- adapters are read-only in this phase
- adapters do not mutate live state
- adapters do not execute tools
- adapters do not call model runtimes
- adapters do not perform live retrieval from FORGE-K
- adapters do not feed RAG results into user-visible responses
- adapters may normalize live records into simulator evidence refs
- adapters may produce dry-run reports
- adapters must preserve provenance
